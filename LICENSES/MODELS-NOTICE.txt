Washa — AI models and components used (notice)
==============================================

Washa uses machine-learning models and libraries made by others. Each keeps its own license.
The license texts are in this same folder (shown in the app under Settings > "使っているモデルとライセンス").
Which items a build contains depends on the platform: the Windows zip carries items 1-5 in its models folder or its libraries,
the Android app uses items 1-5 (the models are fetched at first use or placed by hand), and the iPhone / Mac apps use items 5 and 6.
Retrieved 2026-10-01 (item 6: 2026-10-04).

   Model:   SenseVoiceSmall by FunAudioLLM / Alibaba Group (Tongyi SpeechTeam)
            https://github.com/FunAudioLLM/SenseVoice
   Format:  int8 ONNX export by the sherpa-onnx project (k2-fsa), 2024-07-17 release
            https://huggingface.co/csukuangfj/sherpa-onnx-sense-voice-zh-en-ja-ko-yue-2024-07-17
   License: FunASR Model Open Source License Agreement v1.1
            (model-sensevoice-FunASR-MODEL_LICENSE.txt)
   Credit:  "SenseVoiceSmall" — Copyright (C) 2023-2028 Alibaba Group. Model name retained as required by §2.2.
